Attendance Policy

▼
Purpose

This policy outlines the attendance expectations for employees at DKE Parts Private Limited. Punctuality and regular attendance are vital for maintaining productivity, improving collaboration and providing excellent customer service.

All employees are expected to be at their work stations, ready to start work at 9:00 AM sharp until 6:30 PM each day.

Scope

This policy is applicable to all the employees of the company.

Attendance & Punctuality

The General Office is open Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:30 PM. The office is closed on Sundays. If an employee needs to be absent or late, they must notify their Department Head or HR by filling out an online form no more than 30 minutes before their start time.

Penalty For Late Attendance
  • Employees are allowed to arrive late up to 3 times per month between 9:00 and 11:00 AM without penalty.
  • Employees are allowed to arrive late once per month up until 1:00 PM without penalty.
  • Employees are allowed to leave early once per month after 2:00 PM without penalty.
  • After 3 late arrivals in a month, the employee will receive a half-day leave deduction penalty.
  • Additional late arrivals beyond what is allowed per month will result in further disciplinary action.

If an employee does not have any paid leave or sick leave available, any used leave will be deducted from the following month's salary.

In case an employee does not punch the card either at the time of reaching or leaving the office, she/he will be considered as absent for that particular day.

Half Day Timings

The minimum timings for a half day are — 9:00 to 1:00 or 2:00 to 6:30.

Absence from duty during Official Tour

If an employee needs to be absent from duty during an official tour, it is typically required to notify their Reporting Manager/HR providing a valid reason for the absence.

Absence From Duty
  • Unapproved absence days will be counted as loss of pay (LOP) and the employee's pay will be deducted accordingly.
  • Upon returning to work after an unapproved absence, the employee must immediately report to their department head or HR and provide a written explanation.
  • If an employee is continuously absent for more than 5 days (including weekends/holidays), HR will send official correspondence requiring them to report back to duty.
  • If the employee does not respond within the stipulated time, it will be assumed they have voluntarily resigned and their employment will be terminated.

Mobile Phone Policy

▼
Purpose

To provide company-owned mobile phones to employees whose roles require direct communication with clients and vendors, ensuring professional and efficient business operations.

Eligibility

Phones will be issued strictly to employees who manage external communication with vendors/clients, or require mobility for field operations (usage more than 1 hour).

Usage Guidelines
  • Phones are for official business use only
  • Personal use must be limited and reasonable
  • Unauthorized app installations are not allowed
  • International or roaming calls require prior written approval
Responsibility & Return
  • Employees must ensure proper care and security of the device
  • Loss or damage must be reported to HR within 24 hours
  • Device must be returned upon resignation, transfer, or role change
  • Cost deduction will apply in case of loss or negligent damage
Monitoring & Non-Compliance

Usage will be monitored periodically. Misuse may lead to: (1) Verbal/Written Warning, (2) Cost Recovery. The company may install CRM applications, call recording apps and mobile monitoring tools.

Leave Policy

▼
1. Sick Leave (SL)
  1. Employees are entitled to 12 days of Sick Leave per calendar year.
  2. Sick leave is only for the employee's own illness and cannot be used for a family member's health issues.
  3. Sick Leave can be availed only after completion of the probation period and confirmation of employment.
  4. Normally, no more than 3 consecutive days of Sick Leave may be taken at one time.
  5. If Sick Leave exceeds 3 consecutive days, submission of a medical certificate to the HR Department is mandatory.
  6. In case of casualty of an immediate family member (father, mother, spouse, or child), Sick Leave up to 6 days may be permitted, subject to submission of valid supporting documents and prior approval from HR and Management.
  7. Sick Leave is not permitted for self-marriage or child birth and must be applied under the appropriate leave category.
  8. If an employee does not have sufficient Sick Leave balance, Privilege Leave may be utilized.
  9. If both Sick Leave and Privilege Leave balances are insufficient, the remaining absence will be treated as Leave Without Pay (LWP).
  10. Any holiday or weekly off falling during a Sick Leave period will be counted as Sick Leave.
  11. Unused Sick Leave will lapse on 31st December of each calendar year.
  12. Employees must inform their reporting manager and HR on the first day of Sick Leave, through phone, message, or the HR portal.
2. Privilege Leave (PL) Policy
  1. Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid Privilege Leave (PL) per calendar year.
  2. Privilege Leave must be applied for and approved in advance by Management.
  3. National holidays, declared holidays, festival days, and weekends may be combined with Privilege Leave.
  4. Any holidays falling during a period of Privilege Leave will be counted as Privilege Leave days.
  5. Privilege Leave must be taken in full-day increments only; half-day PL is not permitted.
  6. Employees may carry forward up to 12 unused Privilege Leave days to the next calendar year.
  7. Any Privilege Leave taken beyond the annual entitlement of 12 days, without prior approval, will be treated as Unpaid Leave.
  8. Employees may attend to personal work during office hours, provided the duration does not exceed 2 hours. After 2 hours it will be considered as half day leave.
  9. If the Employee is not present in the office for more than 5 Hours, then it will be considered as a full day leave.
3. Late Coming & Deduction Policy
  1. Three (3) late arrivals — Warning / recorded as late
  2. After 3rd Late, Every Late will result as Half-Day Deduction from PL/CL whatever applicable.
Paternity Leave
  1. Employees have the right to a 7-day leave.
  2. In order to be eligible, an employee must have completed a minimum of 80 days of service within the 12 months leading up to the expected birth or adoption date.
  3. The leave can be used within six months following the birth or adoption of a child.
  4. Failure to utilize the leave within the specified timeframe will result in it being considered forfeited.
  5. Paternity leave is distinct from other types of leave, and it does not impact an employee's leave balance.
  6. Employers are not permitted to reject an employee's request for paternity leave.
Cancellation of Leave

A department head may revoke approved leave if required by business needs or situations. If an employee proceeds to take leave that has been canceled, those days will be treated as unapproved absences.

Extension of Leave

Employees must obtain approval in advance for any extension of leave. To extend leave, the employee should apply to their department head well in advance. If an employee takes leave beyond the approved period without sanctioned extension, those days will be treated as unapproved absences.

Reschedule of Leave

To maintain operational efficiency, the general policy is that only one employee per department may take leave at the same time. The Managing Director, HR department, or Reporting Manager can deny or reschedule leave requests if there is a conflict between two employees from the same department.

Exception

This leave policy is subject to change, amendment, or alteration by management at any time based on business or legal requirements. For any policy clarification, the interpretation of the HR department or the Managing Director shall be final.

OT Policy — Compensation Leave

▼

Employees who work on weekends or holidays are eligible for compensation leave as follows:

Hours WorkedEntitlement
Less than 3 hoursNot eligible for comp leave; eligible for minimum conveyance allowance if approved by manager
3–5 hoursHalf-day compensation leave
Over 5 hoursOne full-day compensation leave
  • Compensation leave expires in 60 days and must be used within 60 days of the date worked (inclusive).
  • Compensation leave cannot be encashed, transferred, or accumulated.
  • Compensation leave requires prior approval from manager/HR. If not approved, leave will be treated as non-medical leave.
  • Only one employee per department can take compensation leave at a time.
  • Employees must mention "compensation leave" when submitting online leave applications.

Travel & Tour Policy

▼

This Travel Policy is applicable to all employees traveling for official business.

1. Travel Authorization
  • All official travel must be pre-approved by the reporting manager.
  • Employees must submit a travel request with estimated expenses before departure through the Google Form.
2. Travel Allowances
A. Transportation
  • Air Travel: Economy class (allowed only in special cases)
  • Train Travel: Sleeper class for junior staff; 3rd AC Economy for mid-level employees; 3rd AC for senior-level employees
  • Rapido Bike / Taxi / Car Rental: Allowed for local travel at the destination; Receipts are mandatory
  • Public Transport (Metro / Bus / Auto): Reimbursed on actuals; Receipts required
B. Accommodation

Budget accommodation will be booked by the office. In special cases, if employees book on their own, prior approval from management is mandatory.

C. Daily Allowance (Food & Incidental Expenses)
LevelPer Day Allowance
Junior Staff₹500
Mid-Level Employees₹700
Senior-Level Employees₹1,000
D. Other Expenses

Minor official expenses will be reimbursed on actuals against bills (receipts required).

3. Reimbursement Process
  • All claims must be submitted within 7 days of return with valid receipts.
  • Expense reports must be approved by the reporting manager before processing.
  • Advances can be provided if pre-approved and must be properly settled after return.
4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Personal expenses will not be covered by the company.
